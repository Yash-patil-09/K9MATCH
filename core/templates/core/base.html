The K9Match 4-Point Ethical Welfare Charter

Prioritizing canine genetics, safety, and lifelong welfare.

1. Zero-Tolerance Puppy Mill Ban

Commercial puppy factories and unverified backyard breeding operations are strictly prohibited.

2. Mandatory Veterinary Clearance

Up-to-date core vaccinations (Rabies, DHPPiL) and pre-mating vet fitness certification by registered clinics.

3. Maternal Health & Age Safeguards

Minimum breeding age of 18-24 months with mandatory rest intervals between litters to safeguard the mother.

4. Verified Lineage & Inbreeding Protection

Verifiable pedigree parentage and inbreeding controls to prevent genetic bottlenecks and uphold vitality.

Recognized Health Registry Standards

The world standard in certified pedigree authentication, genetic health screening, and ethical canine breeding management. Protecting breeds for future generations.
